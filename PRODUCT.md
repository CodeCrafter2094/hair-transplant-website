# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is English-speaking prospective hair-transplant patients, especially people in the United Kingdom comparing treatment in Antalya. They need to understand whether the clinic is credible, what the decision process involves, and how to begin a private assessment quickly.

## Product Purpose

The website turns organic-search and campaign traffic into qualified WhatsApp enquiries for Turkey hair-transplant treatment in Antalya. Success means a visitor can understand the offer, evaluate the process, and start a useful conversation without pressure.

## Positioning

The experience leads with an inspectable, personalised planning process rather than generic transformation promises: the visitor sends current hair photos and goals, then discusses a suitable plan with the clinic team.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive while comparing clinics, techniques, travel logistics, recovery, and likely suitability. Most initial conversions happen on mobile and continue in WhatsApp at **+44 7988 487251**.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing stack: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind, GSAP, Lenis and a lazily loaded Three.js hero scene.
- Primary conversion channel: WhatsApp.
- Core techniques referenced by the current project: FUE, DHI and Sapphire FUE.
- The exact clinic address, surgeon identity, accreditations, package inclusions, prices, outcome statistics and response-time commitments are not yet confirmed and must not be fabricated.
- Search visibility is a goal, but no first-position ranking can be guaranteed.

## Brand Commitments

- Public-facing name: Turkey Hair Transplant Antalya.
- Binding reference domain: https://turkeyhairtransplantantalya.com/
- The supplied design documents are binding source material and should be harmonised rather than copied literally.
- Voice: direct, calm, premium, transparent and conversion-focused.

## Evidence on Hand

- Four supplied design-system documents: `design1.md` through `design4.md` in `C:/Users/gokha/.copilot/repos/md/`.
- One generated campaign-style hero image at `public/assets/antalya-hair-transplant-hero.png`; it is illustrative and must not be presented as a real patient result.
- No verified patient reviews, before-and-after cases, doctor profile, awards or clinic certifications are currently present.

## Product Principles

- Explain the decision before asking for commitment.
- Use only claims that can be supported.
- Make WhatsApp the shortest path, but never the only path to understanding.
- Treat privacy, candidacy and realistic expectations as conversion assets.
- Build search authority with useful, specific content rather than keyword repetition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic structure, sufficient contrast and reduced-motion behaviour are required. Medical copy must avoid guaranteed outcomes and should encourage individual clinical assessment.
